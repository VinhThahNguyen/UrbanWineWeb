Requirement Overview 1.1 Context Diagram 1 . 2 User Requirements 1.2.1 Actors # Actor Description 1 Customer (Visitor/Buyer) A person who visits the website to browse, search, and buy wine. They verify that they are 18 years or older, manage items in their shopping cart, enter delivery address details, select a payment method (VietQR, VNPay, or COD), and track their order history and status. 2 Admin (Store Manager) The store manager who runs the website and business operations. They log in to manage wine products (CRUD details, price, and stock), approve pending orders, assign delivery tasks to shippers, and view inventory stock alerts. 3 Shipper (Delivery Person) Internal delivery staff who delivers wine to local customers. They log in to view their assigned orders, check customer contact and address information, collect cash for COD orders, and update the delivery status (COMPLETED or CANCELLED). 4 Payment Gateway (External System) An external automated payment system (such as VietQR or VNPay Sandbox). It receives payment initialization requests from the website, processes transactions, and sends real-time Webhook IPN notifications (SUCCESS or FAILED) back to the system. 1.2.2 Use Cases In which ID Feature Use Case Description 01 Product Catalog View Wine Catalog Allows customers to browse available wines and view product details such as alcohol content (ABV), origin, vintage, price, and stock status. 02 Product Catalog Search Wine Catalog Allows customers to search for specific wines and apply filters by price range, alcohol percentage (ABV), origin country, and wine type. 03 Cart Operations Manage Cart Allows customers to add wine products to their shopping cart, update item quantities, or remove items before checkout. 04 Checkout Place Order Allows customers to enter delivery address, recipient contact information, and submit a new wine purchase order. 05 Payment Select Payment Method Allows customers to choose an intended payment method ( VietQR dynamic, VNPay gateway, or Cash on Delivery) to initialize payment. 06 Order Tracking View Order History Allows logged-in customers to view their past orders, check order details, and track real-time delivery status. 07 Wine Catalog Manage Wine Catalog Allows the store manager (Admin) to add new wine items, edit product information (price, ABV, origin), update stock quantities, or delete products. 08 Order Processing Process Order Allows Admin to review pending customer orders, approve them for processing, or cancel orders with an attached reason. 08.1 Prepaid Order Rejection Process Order Allows the Store Administrator to reject an order that has already been paid online. Upon rejection, the system automatically issues a full refund to the customer's original payment method, updates the payment status to 'Refunded', restores the ordered items back to the available product inventory, records a mandatory cancellation reason, and notifies the customer. 09 Order Delivery Assign Order to Shipper Allows Admin to select processed orders and assign them to an available delivery person (Shipper). 10 Inventory Control View Stock Alert Allows Admin to monitor low-stock and out-of-stock wine items to ensure timely inventory replenishment. 11 Delivery Operations View Assigned Order Allows shippers to log in and view a list of delivery tasks and order details assigned to them by the Admin. 12 Delivery Operations Update Delivery Status Allows shippers to mark an order as completed or cancelled, and record cash collection for COD orders. 13 Payment Operations Display Payment Method Allows shippers to display a VietQR code or payment details on their mobile app screen for customers to scan and pay upon delivery. 1.2.3 Business rules ID Category Rule Definition BR-01 Constraints Age Verification: Customers must verify that they are 18 years or older before browsing wine products or placing orders. BR-02 Constraints Delivery Scope: The system only accepts and processes delivery orders with addresses located within the designated inner-city area. BR-03 Computations Order Total Calculation: Total order amount = Sum of (wine item unit price * quantity) + inner-city delivery fee. BR-04 Constraints Order Cancellation Rights: Customers can cancel an order directly on the system only when the order status is PENDING (Awaiting Approval). When the status is PROCESSING or SHIPPING, cancellation must be requested through and approved by the Store Manager (Admin). BR-05 Constraints Product Management Authority: Only Store Managers (Admin) have permission to add new wines, edit product details, update prices, and adjust inventory stock quantities. BR-06 Action Enablers Low Stock Alert: When the stock quantity of a wine product reaches or falls below the minimum threshold, the system automatically sets the stock status to LOW_STOCK and displays an alert to the Admin. BR-07 Constraints Online Payment Confirmation: For online payments ( VietQR or VNPay ), an order is updated to PROCESSING status only after the system receives a real-time successful payment confirmation from the payment gateway. BR-08 Constraints Shipper Order Access: Shippers can only view order details and update the delivery status of orders that are assigned to their own account. BR-09 Action Enablers COD Payment Settlement: When a Shipper updates a Cash on Delivery (COD) order status to COMPLETED (Delivery Successful), the system automatically updates the payment status to PAID. BR-10 Constraints/action enable If an order that has already been paid online is rejected by the Store Administrator or cancelled, the system must automatically issue a full refund to the customer's original payment method, update the payment status to 'Refunded', restore the ordered items back to the available product inventory, and record a mandatory cancellation reason. 1.2.4 Main work flow 1.2.4.1 Order to Cash 1.2.4.2 Fulfilments &amp;
